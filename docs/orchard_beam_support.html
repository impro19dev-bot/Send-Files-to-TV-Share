Send Files to TV: Share sends files from your iPhone to a browser on the same Wi‑Fi.

  1. Connect the iPhone and the television to one network. Pause a VPN on the phone.
  2. Open Send to TV, choose files, and start sharing. Leave the app in front.
  3. On the television, open its web browser and type the address shown on the iPhone.
  4. Stop sharing when the downloads finish.

The square code is a copy of the address. The television browser does not scan it. Planning screens illustrate typical cases; they do not measure your network.
